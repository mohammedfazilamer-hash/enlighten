## Voiceover Script

Study material rarely arrives ready to learn. It might be a screenshot, a photo of the board, a dense PDF, or a paragraph you cannot make sense of yet.

Enlighten turns that material into a guided lesson. I can paste text, choose several screenshots, take a photo, or import a document. The text is extracted directly on my Android phone.

When I tap Read aloud, an installed offline voice begins speaking. Enlighten highlights each word and gives me sentence controls, speed adjustment, pause, and resume.

At the same time, my private local AI prepares a simpler explanation. When the original text ends, the app can continue directly into that explanation, so listening becomes understanding without another search or copy-and-paste step.

The same source becomes editable flashcards, a self-check quiz, and an Ask Tutor conversation grounded in the passage. I can save everything as one study set and return to it later.

The phone handles OCR, documents, speech, and storage. AI runs through Ollama and Llama 3.2 on my own computer, so there is no paid inference API and no cloud OCR upload.

I built Enlighten with Codex powered by GPT-5.6. It implemented and tested the Android architecture, OCR pipeline, speech state machine, local inference, persistence, and real-device fixes, while I directed the learning experience and privacy decisions.

Enlighten makes almost any material something a student can hear, understand, and practice.

## Editing Notes

- Use the real app. Do not replace core interactions with mockups.
- Crop the vertical phone capture into a stable phone frame on a 16:9 canvas.
- Keep the phone large enough for text to remain readable on YouTube.
- Remove waits longer than one second unless demonstrating local model latency.
- Use clean hard cuts and restrained text transitions; avoid decorative effects.
- Keep background music quiet enough that every voiceover word is clear.
- Never show the personal LAN address, profile photo, Android notification shade, or unrelated applications.
- Show Codex for no more than ten seconds. The project must remain the main character.
