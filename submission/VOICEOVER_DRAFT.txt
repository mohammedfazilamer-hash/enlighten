Studying rarely begins with clean, accessible text. Notes arrive as screenshots, camera photos, scanned pages, PDFs, documents, and copied passages.

Enlighten turns all of that material into one continuous study flow on Android. The phone extracts text on device, then reads it aloud with an installed offline voice. Live word highlighting, speed control, pause, and sentence navigation help the learner stay with the passage.

When the reading ends, Enlighten can continue into a private AI explanation. Only the study text travels across the learner's trusted network to Ollama running Llama 3.2 on their own computer. There is no paid inference API and no cloud OCR upload.

The explanation becomes active practice immediately. Enlighten identifies important terms and key points, generates editable flashcards, opens a quiz mode, and keeps an Ask Your Tutor conversation grounded in the same source. A complete session can be saved as a study set on the phone.

I built Enlighten with Codex powered by GPT-5.6. It implemented and tested the Android architecture, OCR pipeline, speech state machine, local inference, persistence, and real-device fixes, while I directed the learning experience and privacy decisions.

Enlighten makes almost any material easier to hear, understand, and practice, while keeping the student in control of their data.
