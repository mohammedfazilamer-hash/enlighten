# Enlighten Animated Opening

**Target duration:** 81 seconds  
**Style:** Hand-drawn whiteboard story with graphite linework, subtle teal and yellow accents, left-to-right drawing reveals, gentle camera movement, and short chapter captions.

## Story Arc

| Time | Chapter | Visual story | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:10 | The long day | A rideshare driver finishes a shift, safely parked as evening settles over the city. A college backpack and cybersecurity book wait on the passenger seat. | `THE LONG DAY` |
| 0:10-0:21 | No energy left | At home, the same student sits at a desk with an open laptop and textbook, exhausted but still wanting to learn. | `MY BOOKS WERE READY. I WASN'T.` |
| 0:21-0:30 | The idea | Notes, a phone, headphones, and an open book connect in the student's sketchbook. The student looks up with a new idea. | `WHAT IF STUDY COULD MOVE WITH ME?` |
| 0:30-0:42 | Study in transit | Maya rides a bus as a passenger, listening to imported notes. The campus approaches outside the window. | `REVIEW ON THE WAY` |
| 0:42-0:53 | Keep the workout | Jordan loads a reading before exercising, locks the phone, and listens on a stationary bike. | `KEEP THE WORKOUT. KEEP THE LESSON.` |
| 0:53-1:04 | Learn through chores | Sam folds laundry with a parent while listening to a chapter from a phone resting safely on a shelf. | `CHORES DONE. CHAPTER REVIEWED.` |
| 1:04-1:12 | Understand | Aisha moves from a difficult passage to a simple explanation and organized key ideas. | `FROM WORDS TO MEANING` |
| 1:12-1:21 | Active practice | Aisha uses flashcards, quiz questions, and a tutor response grounded in the source. | `HEAR. UNDERSTAND. PRACTICE.` |

## Story And Claim Notes

- The origin story is first-person and based on the creator's experience as an Uber driver entering cybersecurity college.
- Transit use is shown only for passengers and public transportation. The app should never be operated while driving.
- The workout scene shows the material being loaded before exercise and the phone remaining locked during movement.
- The chores scene frames the benefit as fitting learning into family life, not as a guarantee against distraction.
- The final chapter demonstrates product capabilities without claiming that the app guarantees higher grades.

## Transition To Product Demo

The final sketch phone expands to fill the frame. A short title card reads `THIS IS ENLIGHTEN`, then the existing 90-second app demonstration begins without repeating the origin story.
